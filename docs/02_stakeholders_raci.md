## Elicitation agenda and open decisions

These are questions for validation, rather than recorded stakeholder answers.

1. Does the customer promise contain a day only, and which local timezone governs a breach?
2. What operational action can staff take before the promise expires?
3. Which event distinguishes carrier handoff from a label being created?
4. Can a single order be split across packages, sellers or carriers?
5. How frequently do source statuses arrive and how are backdated updates handled?
6. How many cases can each operations owner resolve per working day?
7. Who accepts, escalates and closes an exception, and within what response time?
8. Which order statuses should leave the queue after cancellation or delivery?
9. What event proves that an intervention was attempted and completed?
10. Which communication channels are consented and technically available?
11. Which late outcomes can reasonably be influenced by the pilot?
12. How should sellers challenge a score or dispute shipment responsibility?
13. Which financial amounts represent customer charges, seller payouts and operating costs?
14. What minimum cohort size and detectable effect would justify pilot expenditure?
15. How should seasonal volume and equal follow-up be handled?
16. What service cost, cancellation or ETA inflation would stop the pilot?
17. Who owns semantic-model release, refresh failures and metric changes?
18. Which source-license terms govern publication of raw and embedded report data?

## Communications and approvals

During discovery, BA and BI review definition and lineage issues together. During a live pilot, logistics reviews queue capacity daily, CX reviews resolution quality weekly, and the sponsor reviews outcome and cost at the agreed decision gate. These cadences are proposed.

The decision record captures decision ID, options, evidence, selected option, accountable owner, date, conditions and expiry. Current records are D-001 calendar-date analytical assumption, D-002 pilot scope and D-003 publication conditions. D-001 is adopted for this portfolio baseline. Operational acceptance of all three remains pending.
